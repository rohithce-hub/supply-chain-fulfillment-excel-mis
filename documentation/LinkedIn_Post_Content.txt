🚀 Excited to share my latest Advanced Excel project: an executive Supply Chain & E-Commerce Order Fulfillment MIS Dashboard!

Order fulfillment velocity and delivery SLA compliance are critical operational levers for any modern retail business. In this project, I developed an end-to-end operational MIS dashboard to monitor 1,500 shipment dispatches, evaluate carrier transit variances, and diagnose fulfillment bottlenecks across 10 commercial distribution hubs.

📊 Dataset Scope & Engineering Decision:
While the raw Kaggle DataCo Global Supply Chain Dataset spans 180,519 rows and 53 columns (~96 MB), loading that volume directly into an Excel workbook creates heavy recalculation lag and sluggish slicer filtering. 

To achieve instantaneous, sub-second client interactivity while maintaining statistical fidelity, I curated a representative 1,500-order sample (Jan – Oct 2026). This sample precisely mirrors the macro proportions of the parent dataset—including the ~78% fulfillment SLA rate, lead-time variances across 4 shipping tiers, and product category demand.

🛠️ Key Excel Engineering Highlights:
• 3-Sheet Model Architecture: Clean separation between raw data (OrdersTable), analytical computation (Pivot_Calcs), and executive presentation (Dashboard).
• Synchronized Multi-Slicer Report Connections: 3 interactive Slicers (Fulfillment Status, Shipping Mode, Warehouse Hub) linked via Report Connections across 6 underlying PivotTables for synchronous multi-visual drill-downs.
• Dynamic KPI Engine: Summary KPI cards powered by dynamic =GETPIVOTDATA() formulas with IFERROR wrappers, ensuring 100% calculation consistency and zero #REF! errors during filtering.
• Advanced Multi-Metric Visuals: Clustered column category SLA breakdown, dual-line monthly order velocity vs. on-time fulfillment trend, delivery lead-time variance analysis, and horizontal hub rankings.
• Publication-Ready Formatting: Gridline suppression, high-contrast dark navy palette, custom currency formatting, and suppressed PivotChart field buttons.

🔍 Key Business Insights:
1. The SLA Gap: Achieved a 78.3% on-time fulfillment rate (1,175 orders), leaving 15.7% (236 orders) delayed.
2. Carrier Transit Variance: Same Day (0.1 d avg) and First Class (1.6 d avg) demonstrated near-perfect compliance, whereas Standard Class accounted for 88% of all delayed shipments.
3. Regional Demand Hubs: Bengaluru (300 orders), Kochi (260 orders), and Mumbai (214 orders) account for over 51.6% of dispatched volume, identifying prime locations for regional micro-fulfillment center expansion.
